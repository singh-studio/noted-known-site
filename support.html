Contact

Email kris@singhstudio.co.nz. We read every message. There is no account and no login, so just tell us which iPhone you have and what you are seeing.

Your data

Where it lives

Everything you write stays on your device. There is no account and no Noted & Known cloud copy of your people or notes.

Export a copy

In the app, you can export everything as a JSON file at any time. It is a point-in-time snapshot, so store it somewhere private.

Delete your data

In the app, Settings → Clear all data removes the people, notes, chronicles and settings stored on your device. To ask us to delete any crash reports or analytics associated with your install, email kris@singhstudio.co.nz.

Noted & Known Full

Noted & Known is free for up to eight people. Noted & Known Full is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited people, custom labels, saved views and five more skins. It is never a subscription.

Restore a purchase

If you reinstall or move to a new device on the same Apple Account, open the upgrade sheet (Settings → Noted & Known Full, or by adding a ninth person) and choose Restore to bring your purchase back at no charge.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by the App Store. Refund requests go through Apple directly, under their refund policies.

Common questions

Reminders aren't showing up

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device. Make sure notifications are allowed for Noted & Known in your phone's system settings, and that the person still has the date or reminder set.

I lost my data after reinstalling

Noted & Known keeps everything on your device and has no cloud sync, so deleting the app removes its on-device data. Export regularly if you keep notes that matter to you. Restoring from an export is manual and replaces what is on the device.

Is the app lock encryption?

No. The biometric or passcode lock is a barrier on the app's screen, not database encryption. See the privacy policy for the full, plain detail.
