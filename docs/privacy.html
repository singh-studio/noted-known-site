Privacy.

Noted & Known keeps personal notes local. This page explains what the app stores, which permissions it can ask for, and what stays on your device.

At a glance

Your people, notes, moments, birthdays, and settings are stored on your device. There is no Noted & Known cloud copy of your relationship data. The app sends nothing about how you use it unless you turn on “Usage and crash reports” in Settings, which is off when you install it.

What the app stores

Noted & Known stores the information you add inside the app:

  • people you add
  • circles
  • notes and gift ideas
  • chronicles
  • birthdays you enter
  • profile photos you choose
  • settings and preferences

This information lives in a local database on your device.

Permissions

Notifications

Noted & Known can schedule local reminders for check-ins and birthdays. They’re created by your device, not pushed from a server.

Contacts

Noted & Known asks for Contacts access only if you choose to import contacts. You choose which people to add, and the import is one-time. It copies the person’s name, contact photo, and birthday into the local database on your device, and nothing is uploaded.

Photos

Noted & Known may ask for photo library access if you choose a profile image. It doesn’t scan your photo library or upload it to a server.

Export

Export creates a point-in-time JSON snapshot of your app data. The file is plain text and isn’t encrypted by Noted & Known.

Once you save or share an export outside the app, its privacy depends on where you put it. Treat it like sensitive personal data.

Noted & Known doesn’t provide its own cloud backup or restore. On iPhone, your local app data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, which is made and encrypted by Apple under your Apple Account, not by us. On Android, the app opts out of system auto-backup.

Usage and crash reports

This is off when you install the app, and nothing about how you use it is sent anywhere until you turn it on. The switch is called “Usage and crash reports” and you’ll find it in the app under You, then Settings, then Privacy.

When it’s on, the app sends anonymous usage events, such as which screens get opened, which features get used, how long a session lasts, the app version and rough count ranges. These events are tied to a random id that the app makes the first time it sends one, and the id isn’t connected to your name, your contacts or anything else about you. If the app crashes or freezes, it also sends a technical report with the stack trace, the app version, your device model and your OS version. Crash reports are processed by Sentry on our behalf and are used only to diagnose and fix problems.

None of this ever includes your people, notes, chronicles, search text, birthdays, contact details, photos, screenshots, exports or your device’s personal name, and the app has no accounts to identify you by.

When you turn the switch off, the app stops sending, discards the random id and deletes any crash reports still waiting on your device. Clear all data turns it off too. If you’d like us to delete what we received while it was on, see Deleting your data below.

Important limits

  • App lock is a UI barrier, not database encryption.
  • Export is not encrypted.
  • Export is not sync or restore.
  • Device security still matters.
  • Clear all data doesn’t delete exported files you saved elsewhere.

Deleting your data

Everything you write in Noted & Known stays on your device. To delete it, open the app, go to You, then Settings, then Clear all data, or delete the app. Exported files you saved elsewhere aren’t affected.

If you’ve turned on Usage and crash reports, the app will have sent anonymous usage events and crash reports that aren’t linked to you. To have the ones from your install deleted, email kris@singhstudio.co.nz and we’ll delete them within 30 days. Nothing is kept after that.

Contact
