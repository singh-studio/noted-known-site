What Noted & Known is

Noted & Known helps you remember small details about people you care about and set gentle reminders for moments that matter. Data you enter is stored locally on your device. There’s no account system and no Noted & Known cloud for your relationship data.

The app evolves

  • Features may change, be added, or behave differently across updates.
  • Updates may change how stored data is handled.
  • Terms may change as the product matures.

Because your data lives on your device, export a copy you care about before major device changes or updates.

Your content is yours

You’re responsible for the names, notes, gift ideas, chronicles, and other information you type or import. Noted & Known stores that data locally. You own it. Noted & Known doesn’t own it, access it, or back it up.

Device security matters

Use a device passcode. Keep your device physically secure. Don’t leave the app open on a shared or unlocked device.

The biometric lock in Noted & Known is a UI access barrier, not database encryption. Exported files are plain text and aren’t encrypted.

Use it for genuine relationships

Noted & Known is designed for one person’s private use on their own device. Don’t use it in ways that pressure or harm someone.

No guarantee of recovery

Noted & Known is provided as-is. It doesn’t guarantee data recovery after device loss, clear-all-data, reinstall, or a failed update. Export is your best safeguard against data loss.
